Inspection Services

Garment Inspection

Independent garment inspection services designed to evaluate product quality, workmanship, measurements, appearance and packing at different production stages.

Product coverage

  • Apparel and garments
  • Denim and woven garments
  • Knitwear
  • Sportswear
  • Socks and hosiery
  • Children’s wear

Inspection stages

Inspection can be arranged at one stage or at several stages of the same order.

  • PPCPre-Production CheckReview materials, specifications, samples, production readiness and quality requirements before bulk production.
  • IPCInitial Production CheckCheck initial production output, workmanship, measurements and process setup.
  • DUPRODuring Production CheckMonitor bulk production quality, defects, measurements and corrective actions.
  • FRIFinal Random InspectionEvaluate a selected sample of completed goods against agreed inspection criteria.
  • PSIPre-Shipment InspectionVerify shipment readiness, product quality, packing and shipping requirements.
  • 100%100% InspectionInspect every unit within the agreed inspection scope.
  • RERe-InspectionVerify corrections after an earlier inspection or rejection.
  • IN-LINEIn-Line Quality CheckMonitor workmanship and quality during ongoing production.

Main checking points

  • Appearance and workmanship
  • Stitching and seams
  • Measurements and tolerances
  • Fabric and material defects
  • Shade and colour consistency
  • Print, embroidery and trims
  • Labels and branding
  • Packing and assortment
  • Defect classification and results

Reporting and deliverables

  • Inspection summary
  • Defect details and classification
  • Photographic evidence
  • Measurement findings where applicable
  • Result against agreed criteria
  • Corrective-action observations where required

AQL and sampling

The sampling level and acceptance criteria are agreed with the client for each inspection. There is no single universal setting applied to every order.
