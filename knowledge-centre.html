FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which products do you inspect?

Garments (apparel, denim and woven garments, knitwear, sportswear, socks and hosiery, children’s wear), fabrics and materials, home textiles such as towels and bedding, and finished-goods packing and shipments.

At which production stages can an inspection be carried out?

From the Pre-Production Check (PPC) through Initial Production Check (IPC), During Production Check (DUPRO), Final Random Inspection (FRI) and Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI). 100% inspection, re-inspection and in-line quality checks are also available.

How are the sampling level and AQL decided?

The sampling level and acceptance criteria are agreed with the client for each inspection. There is no single universal setting applied to every order.

What does an inspection report include?

An inspection summary, defect details and classification, photographic evidence, measurement findings where applicable, the result against the agreed criteria and corrective-action observations where required.

Can I see a sample report before booking?

Yes. Select the report types on the Sample Reports page and submit the request. Samples are shared privately after the request has been reviewed. Confidential client reports are never published.

Is a factory assessment a certification audit?

No. It is an independent evaluation against scope and criteria agreed with the client. It is not an accredited certification audit.
