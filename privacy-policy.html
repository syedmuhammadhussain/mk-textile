Privacy Policy

How MK Textile Service handles the information you send through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry or a sample-report request, we receive the details you enter in the form: your name, company name, email address, country, the service you are enquiring about, any inspection stage, location, quantity and preferred date you provide, and your message.

How we use it

We use these details only to review your requirements, respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and share requested sample reports. We do not ask for information we do not need for these purposes.

Who receives it

Enquiry details are received by MK Textile Service. Service providers that support the website, such as hosting and email, may process the data on our behalf. This website loads its typeface from Google Fonts.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. If these are introduced, a cookie preference banner will be shown first so that you can accept or reject optional cookies.

Retention and your requests

Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship. You may ask us to correct or delete your details, where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@mktextileservice.com.
