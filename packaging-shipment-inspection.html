Inspection Services

Packaging & Shipment Inspection

Inspection of finished-goods packing and shipment preparation to help verify order presentation, quantity, labelling and loading requirements.

What is verified

  • Packing quality and presentation
  • Assortment and size ratios
  • Barcode and label verification
  • Carton dimensions and condition
  • Carton markings and shipping marks
  • Quantity verification
  • Packing list and order-document checks
  • Container condition and loading supervision
  • Sealing and shipment confirmation

Reporting and deliverables

  • Packing findings
  • Barcode and labelling observations
  • Carton and quantity verification
  • Container photographs
  • Loading evidence
  • Shipment inspection summary
