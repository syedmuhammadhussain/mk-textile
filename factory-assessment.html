Factory Assessment

Factory Assessment & Quality Audit

A structured assessment of manufacturing facilities to evaluate production capability, quality management, infrastructure, operational controls and readiness to meet buyer requirements.

Assessment areas

  • Factory ProfileCompany information, location, products and manufacturing background.
  • InfrastructureBuildings, machinery, production facilities and utilities.
  • Production CapabilityCapacity, production processes and available resources.
  • Quality ManagementQuality procedures, inspection systems, records and controls.
  • Production ProcessesCutting, sewing, finishing, packing and other relevant operations.
  • Compliance & WorkplaceWorkplace conditions, safety practices and applicable requirements.
  • DocumentationProduction, quality, maintenance and operational records.
  • CAPA ManagementCorrective and preventive action systems and follow-up.
  • Production ReadinessAbility to meet proposed product and order requirements.

Assessment workflow

  1. Planning and scope

    Planning and scope confirmation with the client.

  2. Factory visit

    On-site visit and review of the facility.

  3. Evaluation

    Evaluation against the agreed criteria.

  4. Risks and gaps

    Risk and gap identification.

  5. Assessment report

    Findings and evidence in a structured report.

  6. CAPA follow-up

    Follow-up on corrective actions where agreed.

Report contents

  • Factory profile
  • Assessment scope
  • Findings
  • Photographic evidence
  • Quality-system observations
  • Production capability
  • Risk observations
  • CAPA recommendations
  • Overall summary

Scope and criteria

The exact scope and criteria, including any workplace, health and safety or specialised elements, are defined and agreed with the client before each assessment. The assessment is an independent evaluation and is not an accredited certification audit.
