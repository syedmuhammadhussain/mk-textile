About Us

Independent inspection, clearly reported

MK Textile Service provides independent textile inspection and factory assessment services in Pakistan.

Company overview

MK Textile Service provides independent textile inspection and factory assessment services in Pakistan. We support buyers and businesses through structured quality inspections, manufacturing assessments and clear, evidence-based reporting across apparel, fabrics, home textiles and related products.

Our independent approach

Each inspection or assessment is carried out against criteria agreed with the client in advance: the product specification, the sampling level and the acceptance limits. Findings are recorded as observed and supported with photographs and measurements, so the report can be relied on when making a decision about an order or a supplier.

Quality and impartiality commitment

  • Inspection and assessment only, with no sourcing or buying role in the orders we inspect
  • Scope and acceptance criteria agreed before work starts
  • Findings reported factually, with supporting evidence
  • Client reports treated as confidential
