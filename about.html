About MK Textile Service

Experience You Can Trust. Quality You Can Rely On.

MK Textile Service provides independent third-party inspection and factory assessment services in Pakistan, supporting international buyers, brands and businesses in maintaining product quality and production standards.

Who we are

With 30+ years of industry experience, our approach is built on practical textile knowledge, hands-on quality assurance expertise and a strong understanding of manufacturing processes.

We understand that product quality is not just about identifying defects. It is about understanding specifications, monitoring production, evaluating materials and ensuring that products meet agreed requirements before shipment.

From garment and fabric inspections to home textiles, packaging, shipment checks and factory assessments, we provide professional, objective and reliable services tailored to each client’s requirements.

Our commitment

Our commitment is simple: independent inspections, honest findings, clear communication and dependable quality support.

  • Independent inspections
  • Honest findings
  • Clear communication
  • Dependable quality support

Nationwide service coverage

MK Textile Service provides third-party inspection and factory assessment services across Pakistan’s major textile and manufacturing centres. Our service coverage includes Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad, Sialkot, Multan, Islamabad, Rawalpindi and other manufacturing locations across the country.
