Production Monitoring in Garment Manufacturing

Production Monitoring involves continuous or scheduled monitoring of garment production to evaluate workmanship, product quality, production progress and compliance with approved specifications throughout the manufacturing process.

What Is Monitored

  • Monitor Production Quality at Different Manufacturing Stages.
  • Check Workmanship, Stitching, Construction and Measurements.
  • Identify Recurring Defects and Quality Trends.
  • Review Production Output and Quality Performance.
  • Monitor Corrective Actions Taken by the Factory.
  • Highlight Quality Risks and Production Issues.
  • Provide Inspection Findings, Photographs and Monitoring Reports.

Why It Helps

  • Quality Problems Are Identified Early.
  • Corrective Action Can Be Taken While Production Is Still Running.
  • The Risk of Defects Continuing Through Bulk Production Is Reduced.

How It Is Arranged

  • Scheduled Visits or Ongoing Follow-Up During the Production Period.
  • Frequency and Checkpoints Follow the Agreed Scope.
  • Findings Are Reported with Photographs After Each Visit.

Keep in Mind

Production Monitoring complements, and does not replace, a Final Random Inspection of the finished goods.
