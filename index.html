How it works

From enquiry to report in four steps

  1. Send your enquiry

    Tell us the product, the service you need and your preferred inspection date.

  2. Agree the scope

    Inspection criteria, sampling level and acceptance limits are agreed with you.

  3. On-site inspection

    The inspection or assessment is carried out against the agreed criteria.

  4. Receive your report

    Findings, defect classification and photographic evidence in one clear report.
