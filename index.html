How it works

From Enquiry to Report in Four Steps

  1. Enquiry

    Send the product, service, location and preferred inspection date.

  2. Coordination

    Scope, sampling plan, acceptance criteria and schedule are agreed with you.

  3. Inspection

    The inspection or assessment is carried out on site against the agreed criteria.

  4. Reporting

    Findings, defect classification and photographic evidence in one clear report.
