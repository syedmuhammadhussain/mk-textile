Container Services

Container Unloading Inspection

Inspection during container unloading, recording the condition of the container and cartons on arrival.

What is inspected

  • Container identity and seal
  • Condition before opening
  • Internal condition
  • Unloading sequence and handling
  • Wet, crushed, torn or shifted cartons
  • Quantity discrepancies where agreed
  • Visible damage
  • Photographs
  • Final unloading report

Scope of the service

An unloading inspection records what was observed at the time of unloading. It does not guarantee cargo safety and does not replace the responsibilities of the carrier or shipper.

Estimated standard fee

US$200 / Man-Day
